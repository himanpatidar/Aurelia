FAQ

Questions we actually get asked

Sizing, water, guarantees and plating thickness — with real numbers rather than reassurance.

How do I find my ring size?

Indian ring sizes run from about 8 to 26. The reliable method is to measure the inner diameter of a ring that already fits you in millimetres and match it to a conversion chart, or have a jeweller size your finger. Measure at the end of the day when your hands are warm — fingers are measurably smaller in the morning and in cold weather. If you are between sizes, go up: a slightly loose ring is wearable, a tight one is not. We accept exchanges on size within 30 days.

My piece has tarnished. What happens now?

Email us a photo at hello@aurelia.example with your order reference. If it is inside the guarantee window — 12 months on silver and gold-plated brass, 24 months on PVD steel, lifetime on unplated brushed steel — and the cause is normal wear, we replace the piece. We do not ask you to prove how you stored it. Damage from bleach, solvents, abrasive polish, or chlorine on a piece not rated for it is not covered, and physical scratches that have breached the coating are treated case by case.

Can I shower and swim in my jewelry?

It depends on the piece, and we state it on every product page. All our PVD stainless steel is rated for showers, swimming pools and seawater. Rhodium-sealed sterling silver and sealed 18K gold-plated brass are water-safe, though we recommend drying them afterwards. The two exceptions are the Celeste pearl studs and the Astra moonstone pendant: the metal is fine, but pearls and moonstone are porous and should not be soaked.

Is your jewelry safe for sensitive skin?

Every piece is nickel-free. The barrier finish that prevents tarnish also prevents metal salts from reaching your skin, which is the mechanism behind most contact reactions. Our 316L surgical stainless steel is the most tolerated material we work with — it is the same grade used for surgical implants. If you have a diagnosed metal allergy, steel is the safest starting point.

How thick is the plating, really?

Rhodium on our sterling silver pieces is 2 microns. Gold on our plated brass is 1.5 microns of 18K, with hardeners added. PVD on steel is a molecular bond rather than a measurable layer, which is why it outlasts both. For context, plating under 1 micron is decorative and typically fails within weeks of daily wear; we publish these numbers precisely because most brands do not.

Can pieces be re-plated when they wear?

Sterling silver and gold-plated brass can be re-plated indefinitely, and any competent local jeweller can do it. PVD steel cannot be re-coated, because the bond is molecular rather than a surface layer — but it also very rarely needs it. We are working on offering re-plating as a service for pieces past their guarantee.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 working days and delivered in 3–6 working days across India. Shipping is free over ₹1,499 and ₹99 below that. You would receive a tracking link by email on dispatch. Note that this is a demonstration storefront, so no order is actually dispatched.

What is your returns policy?

Thirty days from delivery on anything unworn and in its original packaging, including the pouch and care card. Exchanges for a different ring size are free; other returns are refunded to the original payment method. Earrings can only be returned unopened, for hygiene reasons. The full policy is on our shipping and returns page.

Do you offer gift packaging?

Every order ships in a lined box with a polishing cloth and a care card, at no extra cost — there is no separate gift-wrap upsell. Sets arrive in a larger lined box, and the Noor bridal set comes in a hard-shell travel case.

Is this a real shop?

Not yet. This site is a fully-built demonstration storefront: the catalogue, filters, cart and checkout all work, but there is no payment processor connected, so no money changes hands and nothing ships. Orders are saved in your own browser so you can see the flow end to end.
